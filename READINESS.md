## Generation rule

A project does not need every possible artifact before generation.

### Resume gate
Pass when:
- facts are sufficient;
- ownership is explicit;
- outcome / evidence exists;
- claim boundaries are known.

### Homepage gate
Pass when:
- the story is coherent;
- at least 3 strong projects have visual / clickable proof;
- confidential work can be shown safely or represented in redacted form.
