# Experience Bank

This directory stores raw training environments, not pre-written resume sections.

One meaningful environment / project / role gets one file.

Examples may later include:
- undergraduate chemistry research;
- master's PET / ACP3 research;
- KIN;
- Selection Dictionary;
- internships / employment;
- student organizations / leadership;
- competitions or other substantial projects.

## Ingestion process

For each experience, interview the user until the record can answer:

1. What environment were you in?
2. What was difficult / uncertain about it?
3. What did you personally notice, decide, own, or execute?
4. What did collaborators / AI / tools do?
5. What constraints mattered?
6. What concrete output exists?
7. What external feedback or measured result exists?
8. What failed or changed your mind?
9. What reusable capabilities did this train?
10. Which claims can be independently verified?

Then populate a copy of _TEMPLATE.md.

Do not optimize wording for a JD during ingestion. Preserve facts first.
